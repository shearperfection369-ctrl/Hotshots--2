# Hot Shot TMS · 90-Day Go-to-Market Playbook
**Goal: Move sellability rating 6.5 / 10 → 8 / 10 in 90 days.**

Built by Oliver Cummins · Plymouth, Minnesota · Confidential

---

## The 4 Levers (in priority order)

1. **One paying logo signed** — single biggest force-multiplier
2. **SOC 2 Type I in flight** — opens the door to enterprise procurement
3. **One peer-validated case study** — turns "Oliver claims" into "Sarah at Tennant says"
4. **Two channel-partner LOIs** — unlocks the third-party sales motion

---

## Days 1–30 · "Anchor Logo"

**Theme**: Close Tennant (or one comparable MN industrial) as the first paid logo.

### Week 1 · Internalize the wedge
- [ ] Memorize the 60-second re-theming demo cold. Run it in front of a
      mirror 30 times. The first 30 seconds determine everything.
- [ ] Write a 1-page "Why Hot Shot wins where SAP TM loses" sales sheet —
      mid-market focus, 30-day deploy, opex SaaS, no implementation team.
- [ ] Build a target list: 20 MN-based industrial shippers ($100M–$500M
      revenue), 30 mid-market 3PLs, 10 specialty manufacturers.
- [ ] Schedule a 30-min discovery call with Tennant's VP of Supply Chain.

### Week 2 · Tennant pilot proposal
- [ ] Walk Tennant's VP through the 60-second demo skinned for Tennant.
- [ ] Offer: 90-day paid pilot at **50% discount** ($24K → $12K).
      Conditions: full case-study rights, intro to 2 peer companies, named
      reference in marketing.
- [ ] Drop a 1-page success-criteria contract: "If we don't save 5+ hours
      per week of dispatch time in 30 days, you walk."

### Week 3 · Pilot kickoff
- [ ] Connect Tennant's SAP via the Connections Vault (live demo of
      "2 clicks to plug an ERP" — for real this time, not just the demo).
- [ ] Import their last 6 months of shipment data via CSV.
- [ ] Provision 5 power users (dispatchers + ops VP).
- [ ] Run 2-day training with dispatch team.

### Week 4 · First win signal
- [ ] Capture quantified outcomes:
      - Loads booked through Hot Shot vs. legacy system
      - Time-to-book a load (before vs. after)
      - Carrier-onboarding cycle time
      - BOL/POD turnaround
- [ ] Schedule a video call to record a 90-second customer testimonial.

**End-of-Week-4 deliverable**: Tennant paid invoice + recorded testimonial
clip + 3-bullet case study draft.

## Days 31–60 · "Reference Multiplier"

**Theme**: Convert the Tennant case study into 5 new logo conversations.

### Week 5 · Polish the proof
- [ ] Edit the testimonial into 3 cuts: 30-second LinkedIn version,
      60-second sales-call version, 90-second VC-deck version.
- [ ] Write the formal case study: problem → approach → outcome → quote.
      Distribute on landing page, LinkedIn, and into the data room.
- [ ] Ask Tennant's VP for 2 peer intros (warm — same MN industrial pool).

### Week 6 · SOC 2 kickoff
- [ ] Sign with Vanta or Drata (~$8K–$12K annual). Type I in 30 days,
      Type II in 6 months.
- [ ] Set up the basics: SSO for the app, MFA for admins, encrypted
      backups, access reviews, audit logging (most already done).
- [ ] Add a "Trust Center" page to the marketing site: SOC 2 badge,
      Fernet vault, RBAC, audit log — turns into a sales asset.

### Week 7 · Outbound campaign
- [ ] LinkedIn outbound: 100 personalized messages/week to VP-of-Logistics
      titles at $100M–$500M shippers. Hook: "I built a TMS that re-themes
      itself for your company in 60 seconds — want to see it skinned for
      [their company name] live?"
- [ ] Cold email: 200 emails/week, same hook. Use the personalized invite
      link feature (`/tms-investors?token=…`) — every link gives you a
      visit alert + audit log.
- [ ] Target: 5 qualified discovery calls by end of week.

### Week 8 · Pipeline build
- [ ] Move 2 prospects to demo stage.
- [ ] Move 1 prospect to pilot-proposal stage.
- [ ] Track everything in the existing Hot Shot CRM (or Pipedrive if not yet
      built — see roadmap).

**End-of-Week-8 deliverable**: Tennant case study live + 5 active demos +
1 pilot proposal out + SOC 2 audit started.

## Days 61–90 · "Channel + Round Open"

**Theme**: Open the door to channel partnerships AND open the VC round.

### Week 9 · Channel partner outreach
- [ ] Identify 5 mid-market 3PLs that need a white-label TMS to offer
      their shipper customers (CH Robinson dropdown clones, regional 3PLs).
- [ ] Pitch: "We power your TMS layer. You keep the customer relationship.
      We split the license 70/30 (you / us). You bill on top for services."
- [ ] Goal: 2 signed LOIs by end of week 12.

### Week 10 · VC round open
- [ ] Personalized invite links go out to 25 seed-stage logistics-focused
      VCs (Schematic Ventures, Dynamo, Eniac, Bowery, Fontinalis, NewRoad,
      8VC, etc.).
- [ ] Public investor page goes live with Tennant case study embedded.
- [ ] Target: 15 first calls, 5 second calls, 2 term sheets in 60 days.

### Week 11 · Second paid logo
- [ ] Close the pilot proposal from Week 7 → paid.
- [ ] Same playbook: case study rights, peer intros, named reference.

### Week 12 · 90-day review
- [ ] Score yourself on the original 4 levers:
      - ☐ 1+ paying logo (✅ if Tennant + 1)
      - ☐ SOC 2 in flight (✅ if audit kicked off in week 6)
      - ☐ Peer-validated case study (✅ if Tennant testimonial recorded)
      - ☐ 2 channel LOIs (✅ if 2 signed)
- [ ] Update the sellability rating. Target: **8 / 10.**

**End-of-Week-12 deliverable**: 2 paying logos + 1 case study live +
SOC 2 Type I in audit + 2 channel LOIs + 2 VC term sheets.

## Weekly Cadence (Days 1–90, recurring)

| Day | Activity |
|---|---|
| **Mon AM** | Pipeline review — top 5 prospects, next-step owner, blocker |
| **Mon PM** | LinkedIn outbound batch (50 messages) |
| **Tue AM** | Demo 1 (scheduled) |
| **Tue PM** | Customer Success — Tennant check-in call |
| **Wed AM** | Email outbound batch (100 emails) |
| **Wed PM** | Demo 2 (scheduled) |
| **Thu AM** | Content — 1 LinkedIn post, 1 blog draft |
| **Thu PM** | Demo 3 (scheduled) |
| **Fri AM** | Investor outreach + data room reviews |
| **Fri PM** | Product backlog grooming + next-week prep |

**Discipline**: ≥3 demos/week. ≥150 personalized outbound contacts/week.
≥1 close attempt/week. If those numbers slip 2 weeks in a row,
something is wrong — fix before adding more activity.

## What Could Break the Plan (and what to do)

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Tennant doesn't sign | Pivot anchor to a comparable MN industrial (3M, Polaris, Boston Scientific MN). Same playbook, 30-day slip. |
| First paid customer churns | Built-in by structure — 50% discount + 90-day pilot means you're learning, not selling. |
| Demo magic doesn't translate to a 50K-row data import | Use the implementation-honesty doc. Quote 4–6 weeks instead of "5 days" for real rollout. Customer respects honesty. |
| VC round stalls | Slow it down. Don't take a bad term sheet. Bridge with services revenue from existing logos. |
| Founder is the bottleneck | Hire the founding sales rep at month 4. Budget already in the $1.5M ask. |
