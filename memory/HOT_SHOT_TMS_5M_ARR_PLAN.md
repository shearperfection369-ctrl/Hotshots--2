- **Math**: With 150 personalized outbound contacts/week, conservative 1%
  conversion to demo = 1.5 demos/week. 30% demo → pilot = 0.45 pilots/week.
  50% pilot → paid = ~12 paying logos in Y1 ceiling. Plan for 5 (40% of
  ceiling) accounting for first-12-month learning curve.

### 2. The Channel (Year 2 unlock)
- **Two mid-market 3PL partners** signed in Q3-Q4 Y1, productive in Y2.
- Each partner reaches 50–200 mid-market shippers.
- Even at 1% conversion per partner = 5–10 logos/year from channel.
- 70/30 revenue split (partner keeps customer relationship, we power the TMS).
- **Y2 channel logos: 8–12**, paired with founder-led 10–14 = 22 logos total.

- Adds 2 more business units Year 3 → $60K ACV (2x of Y1).
- **Net revenue retention by Y3: 130–150%.** This compounds.

## What VCs Will Test (and How We Pass)

### "How do you know mid-market shippers will actually pay?"
- Tennant LOI/contract is the proof. By the time we walk into the VC call,
  we want this signed.
- Industry benchmark: TIA reports mid-market TMS spend averages $60K–$200K
  annually. Our $80K–$240K ACV blend sits inside that band.

### "Why won't SAP TM, Oracle OTM, Manhattan, MercuryGate, or BluJay just
copy the re-theming feature?"
- Re-theming alone is copyable in 12 months. But the underlying platform
  (50+ modules, brand-aware document engine, 9 ERPs, 14 integrations,
  Connections vault) takes 3 years to build. By the time they copy the
  surface feature, we have 50 logos and a channel network.
- The wedge buys us a 24-month lead, not a permanent moat. We use that
  lead to land-and-expand into accounts.

### "What if Tennant doesn't sign?"
- Pivot the anchor logo to 3M, Polaris, Boston Scientific MN, or any
  comparable mid-market industrial. 30-day slip, not a fatal hit.
- The MN industrial pool is deep enough that any single "no" doesn't
  break the plan.

### "What's your CAC justification?"
- Y1: $5K CAC via founder-led outbound. Payback in 2 loads ($30K ACV / 6).
- Y3: $15K CAC via channel + content marketing. Payback in 6 loads.
- LTV: $200K (3-year average, 130% NRR). LTV/CAC of 13:1 at scale.

### "Why now? What's changed?"
- SAP/Oracle TM implementations are at all-time-high backlogs (18-month wait).
- Mid-market shipper IT budgets are contracting — they want opex SaaS,
  not capex implementations.
- Claude Sonnet 4.5 finally makes AI-generated brand profiles production-quality.
- Emergent platform reduces SaaS deployment friction by 10x.

## The Honest Risk Disclosure (we say this BEFORE they ask)

- **Implementation isn't actually plug-and-play** for enterprise rollouts.
  It's 4–8 weeks of services per logo. We bill for it (15–25% of ACV).
- **The 60-second demo is the wedge, not the sale.** Win the demo, lose
  the deal in week 3 of discovery = real possibility. Customer success
  engineer mitigates.
- **First sales hire might miss quota by 30%.** Budgeted accordingly.
  Founder picks up slack until rep ramps.
- **One catastrophic logo loss** (bad data, mishandled cutover, churned
  reference) sets us back 6 months. We mitigate with shadow-mode
  pre-production for all enterprise rollouts starting Q3.
- **A well-funded incumbent could copy the wedge in 12 months.** We have
  a 24-month lead. By month 24 we want 30 logos + a channel network to
  defend with land-and-expand economics.
