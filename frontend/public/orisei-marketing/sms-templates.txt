Use these as paired follow-ups to your email outreach.
SMS gets 5-8× the response rate of cold email when timed
24-48 hrs AFTER the email lands.

Replace {Name}, {Carrier}, {Lane}, {Spend} with merge tags.
Keep each under 160 chars (1 SMS segment).
