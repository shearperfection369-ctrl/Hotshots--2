# Operating Agreement
## Orisei Freight Solutions LLC · State of Minnesota · Three Members

**THIS OPERATING AGREEMENT** (this "Agreement") is entered into effective as of the ____ day of ____________, 2026, by and among **Oliver Cummins** (the "**Operator**"), **Daniel W. Karsor**, and **Doug Graham** (each a "**Member**," collectively the "**Members**") of **Orisei Freight Solutions LLC**, a Minnesota limited liability company (the "**Company**"), organized under Minnesota Statutes Chapter 322C. This Agreement operates alongside, and is to be read consistently with, the Members' Partnership Agreement; where the two conflict, the provision more recently executed controls.

---

## 1 · Ownership

**1.1 Equal Stakes.** The Members own the Company in **equal, undivided one-third (33⅓%) membership interests**. No Member's interest is weighted. The Members expressly acknowledge that the differing *forms* of contribution — the Operator's software, business structuring, expenses-to-date, and full-time labor (commitment **PAID IN FULL**, Receipt ORI-RCT-0003); Member Karsor's cash; Member Graham's cash plus twelve years of carrier-side expertise and ongoing advisory labor — are of **agreed equal bargained-for value**, and that equality of ownership is the deliberate foundation of this partnership.

**1.2 Capital Commitments.** Each Member's startup commitment is **$10,000** (aggregate $30,000), tracked member-by-member in the Company's **Capital Accounts ledger** and evidenced by official receipts. Paid-in status as of the Effective Date: Cummins $10,000 (in full, in-kind) · Karsor $2,500 · Graham $1,300; cash balances due per the Phase 0 funding schedule.

**1.3 Labor Equity Protection.** If any Member's ongoing labor becomes materially disproportionate for more than two consecutive quarters, the remedy is **compensation adjustment by unanimous consent** (per §4.4 of the Partnership Agreement) — never unilateral re-weighting of ownership.

---

## 2 · Capital Calls

**2.1 Approval.** No Member may ever be **forced** to contribute additional capital. A capital call (e.g., $50,000 of additional working capital) may be issued only upon **unanimous written approval** of the Members, stating the amount, purpose, per-Member share (pro-rata: one-third each), and a funding deadline of not less than thirty (30) days.

**2.2 If a Member Declines or Cannot Fund — Fair Dilution.** A non-funding Member breaches nothing. The funding Members may cover the shortfall, and Percentage Interests are then adjusted by the **contributed-capital formula**:

> *Adjusted Interest = (Member's total contributed capital, including agreed in-kind values) ÷ (all Members' total contributed capital)*

applied **only to the newly called capital layer** — i.e., dilution is proportional to the shortfall actually covered by others, never punitive, and never triggered by calls smaller than $5,000.

**2.3 Loan Alternative.** In lieu of dilution, the funding Members may unanimously elect to treat the shortfall as a **Member loan** to the Company at a fixed 8% simple annual interest, repaid before distributions resume — leaving all Percentage Interests unchanged. The non-funding Member selects between the dilution and loan mechanics if both are offered.

**2.4 Order of Preference.** Before any capital call, the Company shall first exhaust: retained earnings, the Growth & Continued Operations Account, and commercially reasonable factoring/receivables financing.

---

## 3 · Profit & Loss Split

**3.1** All profits, losses, and distributable cash are allocated **equally — one-third to each Member** — mirroring ownership, subject only to the quarterly Distribution Schedule (operating reserve → Operator salary → **10% reinvestment holdback** credited back to each Member's capital account → equal distributions) and annual tax distributions.

**3.2** If Percentage Interests ever change under §2.2, profit/loss allocations change with them from the effective date forward — never retroactively.

## 5 · Decision-Making

**5.1 Ordinary Course — Operator Authority.** The Operator may unilaterally execute customer contracts, broker-carrier agreements, rate confirmations, and expenses up to **$2,500** per transaction.

**5.2 Majority (two of three Members).** Ordinary decisions outside §5.1: vendor changes, marketing spends, hiring hourly/contract staff, lane strategy.

**5.3 Unanimous (all three Members) — Major Moves.**
- Taking on **any debt** over $5,000; any capital call (§2);
- **Hiring or firing executives** or any salaried role; changing the Operator Salary or holdback percentage;
- **Selling the Company**, merger, sale of substantially all assets, or licensing the platform IP outside the ordinary course;
- Admitting a new Member; amending this Agreement; dissolution;
- Any single contract or commitment over $10,000.

**5.4 Deadlock.** If a majority vote ties through absence or abstention and stays unresolved for 15 days, the matter escalates to §6.

---

## 6 · Dispute Resolution — Mediation First

**6.1** Any dispute goes through, in order: (a) **good-faith negotiation** among the Members (15 days); (b) **mediation** before a neutral mediator in Hennepin County, Minnesota (30 days, costs split three ways); and only then (c) **binding arbitration** — single arbitrator, AAA Commercial Rules, seat in Minneapolis. **No litigation** except to enforce an arbitral award or seek emergency injunctive relief protecting Company IP or authority. Prevailing party recovers reasonable fees at arbitration. Governing law: **Minnesota**.

---

## 7 · Buyout & Exit

**7.1 Voluntary Exit.** A Member wishing to exit gives 90 days' written notice. The Company, then the remaining Members pro-rata, hold a **right of first refusal** (30-day windows each) to purchase the interest.

**7.2 Valuation.** The Company's value is the **greater of**: (a) net asset value (including the platform at replacement cost); or (b) **1.0× trailing-twelve-month gross margin**, in each case determined or confirmed by an independent appraiser the Members jointly select (cost shared). The exiting Member's share = one-third of that value, adjusted for their capital account balance.

**7.3 Payment Terms.** Up to **24 equal monthly installments** at 6% simple interest, prepayable anytime — so a buyout never breaks the Company's working capital.

**7.4 Drag-Along.** If Members holding **two-thirds** approve a bona fide sale of the entire Company to an unaffiliated third party at a uniform price, all Members must sell on identical terms.

**7.5 Tag-Along.** If any Member sells their interest to a third party, the other Members may join the sale **pro-rata on identical terms** — no Member can be left behind with a stranger.

**7.6 Involuntary Events.** Death, permanent disability (180+ days), bankruptcy, or divorce decree affecting an interest triggers the Company's option to redeem at §7.2 value on §7.3 terms.

## 8 · Non-Compete & Non-Solicitation

**8.1 Non-Compete.** During membership and for **two (2) years** after departure, no former Member shall own, operate, or materially assist a **competing freight brokerage** within the Company's market (the Upper Midwest states and any lane generating over 5% of trailing-year revenue).

**8.2 Carve-Out — Member Graham.** Doug Graham's independent **owner/operator trucking activities are expressly permitted** at all times — driving a truck is not brokering freight. He may not, however, divert Company shipper opportunities or use Company data for outside benefit.

**8.3 Non-Solicitation.** For two (2) years after departure, no former Member shall solicit Company customers, carriers, contractors, or employees.

**8.4 Reasonableness.** The Members agree these restraints are reasonable in scope and duration and are severable — a court or arbitrator may blue-pencil rather than void them.
