## 1 · Executive Summary

LiveCleans is the **first Transportation Management System that re-themes itself for any company in minutes**. Type a company name into the admin panel — Claude Sonnet 4.5 generates the brand profile, sample data, ERP context, supplier roster, and lane network. The entire 50-module workspace re-skins instantly. What takes SAP TM, Oracle OTM, and Manhattan 6–18 months of implementation, LiveCleans does in **60 seconds**.

Built by a 13-year logistics practitioner — not a software house — every feature solves a real problem the founder has personally lived running freight at multiple major Minnesota corporations.

**The opportunity:** the $15.3B global TMS market by 2030 (Gartner) has systematically underserved the $100M–$2B mid-market. Enterprise solutions are too heavy; SMB tools lack the modal depth. LiveCleans hits the gap with a **product-led, founder-led, infinitely-themable** offering.
