# Member-Controlled Limited Liability Company Partnership Agreement
## Orisei Freight Solutions LLC · State of Minnesota · Three Members

**THIS OPERATING / PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT** (this "Agreement") is made and entered into effective as of the ____ day of ____________, 2026 (the "Effective Date"), by and among:

- **Oliver Cummins**, an individual residing in Minneapolis, Minnesota ("**Member Cummins**" or the "**Operator**");
- **Daniel W. Karsor**, an individual residing in Brooklyn Park, Minnesota ("**Member Karsor**"); and

## Article I · Formation & Purpose

**1.1 Formation.** The Company is organized under the Minnesota Revised Uniform Limited Liability Company Act, Minnesota Statutes Chapter 322C (the "**Act**"). An amendment to the Company's Articles of Organization admitting Member Karsor and Member Graham as members shall be filed with the Minnesota Secretary of State concurrently with the execution of this Agreement.

**1.2 Name & Principal Office.** The Company shall conduct business as **Orisei Freight Solutions LLC**, with its principal office in the Minneapolis–Saint Paul metropolitan area, Minnesota, and such other places as the Members determine.

**1.3 Purpose.** The Company's purpose is to operate as a licensed **property freight broker** (49 CFR Part 371) — arranging the transportation of freight by vetted motor carriers on behalf of shippers — and to develop, operate, and commercialize related logistics technology, including the proprietary **Orisei Brokerage Command Deck** transportation-management platform, and any other lawful business the Members unanimously approve.

**1.4 Registered Agent.** The Company shall continuously maintain a registered agent in Minnesota as required by the Act.

**1.5 Term.** The Company shall continue perpetually unless dissolved in accordance with Article XII.

## Article III · Allocations, Distributions & Reinvestment

**3.1 Allocation of Profits and Losses.** All items of Company income, gain, loss, deduction, and credit shall be allocated **one-third (33⅓%) to each Member**.

**3.2 Definition — Distributable Cash.** "**Distributable Cash**" for any fiscal quarter means the Company's cash receipts, minus: (a) all operating expenses paid or payable for the quarter (carrier payments, factoring fees, insurance, bond, software, professional fees); (b) debt service; (c) the Operator Salary (§3.6); and (d) the reserves required by §3.3 — as reflected in the quarterly financial statement prepared from the Company's books.

**3.3 Distribution Waterfall.** Distributable Cash shall be determined **quarterly** and applied strictly in the following order (the "**Distribution Schedule**"):

1. **Operating Reserve (first priority).** Retain cash sufficient for **ninety (90) days of operating expenses**, the carrier quick-pay float, BMC-84 surety bond and insurance renewals, and an estimated tax escrow. No distribution may be made that would impair this reserve or violate the solvency limitations of Minn. Stat. §322C.0405.
2. **Company Retained Earnings — 15% (all Members equally).** Fifteen percent (15%) of the quarter's net profit is **retained by the Company** as general retained earnings — the business's permanent war chest. This retention burdens all Members equally by operation of the equal profit split and is not credited to any individual capital account.
3. **Tax Safe-Harbor Distribution.** Distribute to each Member an amount equal to the Member's allocated share of quarterly taxable income multiplied by the **combined highest marginal federal + Minnesota individual rate** (deemed 40% unless the Members unanimously set another rate), so no Member ever funds Company taxes out of pocket.
4. **Reinvestment Holdback — 10% per Member.** From each Member's one-third share of the cash remaining after steps 1–3, **ten percent (10%) is withheld and transferred to the Growth & Continued Operations Account**. Each Member's withheld amount is **credited to that Member's capital account** and may be deployed only for: platform development, marketing and shipper acquisition, compliance and insurance, working capital, or debt reduction. This account is tracked member-by-member in the Company's Capital Accounts ledger.
5. **Member Equity Distributions.** The balance is distributed **equally — 33⅓% to each Member**.

**3.4 How and When Members Are Paid.** Equity distributions are paid as follows:

| Quarter | Books Close | Distribution Statement Issued | **Payment Date (ACH)** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Q1 (Jan–Mar) | March 31 | by April 30 | **on or before May 15** |
| Q2 (Apr–Jun) | June 30 | by July 31 | **on or before August 14** |
| Q3 (Jul–Sep) | September 30 | by October 31 | **on or before November 14** |
| Q4 + annual true-up (Oct–Dec) | December 31 | by January 31 | **on or before February 14** |

- **Method.** Payment by **ACH transfer** to each Member's bank account of record (or check if a Member so elects in writing). Each payment is accompanied by a **Distribution Statement** showing: quarterly net profit; reserve funding; the 15% retained-earnings amount; the Member's tax safe-harbor amount; the Member's 10% Reinvestment Holdback credit; and the net equity distribution.
- **Timing standard.** Payment no later than **forty-five (45) days after quarter close** is the Company's binding standard, consistent with customary practice for closely-held logistics companies.
- **Minimum threshold.** If total distributable equity for a quarter is less than **$1,500**, it rolls forward to the next quarter rather than being paid (avoids de minimis transfers).
- **Equal treatment.** No Member may be paid a distribution unless all Members are simultaneously paid their equal share.
- **Year 1 ramp.** The Members acknowledge Year-1 quarterly distributions will be modest while reserves and the reinvestment accounts are first funded; the waterfall above still applies without exception.

**3.5 Owner Equity Withdrawals (outside the schedule).** A Member may request a withdrawal against their positive capital account balance (excluding the Growth & Continued Operations Account) no more than **once per calendar quarter**, upon **unanimous written consent** of the Members, provided that: (a) the withdrawal does not reduce Company cash below the Operating Reserve in §3.3(1); (b) all Operator Salary and Company obligations are current; and (c) the withdrawal is documented in the Capital Accounts ledger with an official receipt/acknowledgment.

**3.6 Operator Salary — Sole Salaried Member.** The Members agree that **Member Cummins, as Operator and Principal Broker, shall be the only Member to draw a salary** from the Company, structured as a guaranteed payment of **$9,166.67 per month ($110,000.00 per annum)**, commencing the first full month in which Company monthly gross margin exceeds $10,000, and adjustable thereafter only by unanimous written consent. The salary level reflects the Operator's full-time commitment to shipper acquisition, carrier vetting, pricing, and daily operations under the Company's shipper-led growth model. No other Member shall receive any salary, guaranteed payment, wage, or recurring draw; Member Karsor and Member Graham are compensated exclusively through the distributions and withdrawals described in this Article III.

**3.7 Expense Reimbursement.** Members shall be reimbursed for reasonable, documented out-of-pocket expenses incurred on Company business and approved in advance for amounts over $200.

## Article IV · Management & Roles

**4.1 Member-Managed.** The Company is **member-managed**. Except as expressly delegated herein, decisions require the consent of Members holding a **majority** of Percentage Interests; the **Major Decisions** in §4.4 require unanimous consent.

**4.2 Roles.**

| Member | Title | Primary Responsibilities |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Oliver Cummins | Principal Broker / Operator | Daily operations, dispatch, shipper & carrier relations, platform, compliance, banking |
| Daniel W. Karsor | Member — Finance & Growth | Capital strategy, financial oversight, growth initiatives |
| Doug Graham | Member — Capacity & Carrier Relations Advisor | Carrier vetting insight, capacity sourcing, driver-side market intelligence, equipment & lane advisory |

**4.3 Day-to-Day Authority.** The Operator may, without further consent, execute broker-carrier agreements, customer contracts, and rate confirmations in the ordinary course; hire/terminate contractors; and incur ordinary-course expenses up to **$2,500** per transaction.

**4.4 Major Decisions (Unanimous Consent).** (a) admitting a new Member or issuing interests; (b) sale, merger, or dissolution; (c) incurring debt over $5,000; (d) contracts outside the ordinary course over $10,000; (e) changing the Operator Salary or the Reinvestment Holdback percentage; (f) amending this Agreement; (g) selling or licensing the platform IP outside the ordinary course; (h) any distribution other than per Article III.

**4.5 Banking.** Company funds shall be kept in Company accounts and never commingled. The Operator is the primary signer; any single disbursement over $5,000 requires a second Member's written approval.

## Article V · Books, Records & Reporting

**5.1** The Company shall maintain complete books, including capital accounts, the Reinvestment Account ledger, and an official **receipt register** for all capital received. **5.2** Members receive: monthly P&L and gross-margin summary; quarterly distribution statements; annual K-1s. **5.3** Any Member may inspect books on reasonable notice. **5.4** Fiscal year: calendar year. Tax classification: partnership.

---

## Article VI · Intellectual Property

All software, brand assets, data, processes, shipper/carrier lists, and work product created for the Company by any Member are **Company property** (work made for hire; assigned to the extent necessary). The Orisei Brokerage Command Deck platform contributed under §2.4 is Company property from the Effective Date.

---

## Article VII · Duties, Outside Activities & Non-Solicitation

**7.1** Each Member owes the Company duties of loyalty and care under the Act. **7.2** Member Graham may continue his independent owner/operator trucking activities, provided that he shall not divert Company shipper opportunities, and any freight the Company tenders to his equipment shall be at arm's-length market rates disclosed to all Members. **7.3** During membership and for **two (2) years** after, no Member shall solicit Company customers, carriers, or employees for a competing freight brokerage.

---

## Article VIII · Transfer Restrictions

No Member may transfer any interest without: (a) first offering it to the Company, then to the other Members pro-rata, at the same price and terms (right of first refusal, 30-day windows); and (b) unanimous consent for any transferee to be admitted as a Member. Any purported transfer in violation is void. Permitted estate-planning transfers to a revocable trust are allowed with notice.

---

## Article IX · New Members & Additional Capital

Admission of new Members requires unanimous consent and a written joinder. Future capital contributions by any person shall be evidenced by an **official Orisei receipt** stating amount, date, form of funds, and the account credited.

---

## Article X · Insurance & Compliance

The Company shall maintain: contingent cargo and general liability insurance; the **BMC-84 surety bond ($75,000)**; FMCSA broker authority (MC/USDOT) in good standing; and statutory records retention (49 CFR §371.3). The Operator is responsible for compliance calendars; costs are Company expenses senior to distributions.

---

## Article XI · Dispute Resolution

Good-faith negotiation (15 days) → mediation in Hennepin County, MN (30 days) → binding arbitration (single arbitrator, AAA commercial rules, Minneapolis). Prevailing party recovers reasonable fees. This Agreement is governed by **Minnesota law**.

---

## Article XII · Dissociation, Dissolution & Wind-Up

**12.1** On a Member's death, disability, or withdrawal, the Company may redeem the interest at **fair market value** (agreed appraiser; payment over up to 24 months). **12.2** Dissolution requires unanimous consent or judicial decree. **12.3** On wind-up: creditors first; then Reinvestment Account and capital account balances; then remaining proceeds equally.

---

## Article XIII · General Provisions

Entire agreement; amendments in writing signed by all Members; notices by email with confirmation; severability; counterparts and electronic signatures effective; headings for convenience.
