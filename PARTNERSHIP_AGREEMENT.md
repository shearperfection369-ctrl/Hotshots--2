# Member-Controlled Limited Liability Company Partnership Agreement
## Orisei Freight Solutions LLC · State of Minnesota

**THIS OPERATING / PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT** (this "Agreement") is made and entered into effective as of the ____ day of ____________, 2026 (the "Effective Date"), by and between:

- **Oliver Cummins**, an individual residing in Minneapolis, Minnesota ("**Member Cummins**"); and
- **Daniel W. Karsor**, an individual residing in Brooklyn Park, Minnesota ("**Member Karsor**")

(each a "**Member**" and collectively the "**Members**"), with respect to **Orisei Freight Solutions LLC**, a Minnesota limited liability company (the "**Company**").

---

## Article I · Formation & Purpose

**1.1 Formation.** The Company is organized under the Minnesota Revised Uniform Limited Liability Company Act, Minnesota Statutes Chapter 322C (the "**Act**"). An amendment to the Company's Articles of Organization admitting Member Karsor as a member shall be filed with the Minnesota Secretary of State concurrently with the execution of this Agreement.

**1.2 Name & Principal Office.** The Company shall conduct business as **Orisei Freight Solutions LLC**, with its principal office in the Minneapolis–Saint Paul metropolitan area, Minnesota, and such other places as the Members determine.

**1.3 Purpose.** The Company's purpose is to operate as a licensed **property freight broker** (49 CFR Part 371) — arranging the transportation of freight by vetted motor carriers on behalf of shippers — and to develop, operate, and commercialize related logistics technology, including the proprietary **Orisei Brokerage Command Deck** transportation-management platform, and any other lawful business the Members unanimously approve.

**1.4 Registered Agent.** The Company shall continuously maintain a registered agent in Minnesota as required by the Act.

**1.5 Term.** The Company shall continue perpetually unless dissolved in accordance with Article XII.

## Article III · Allocations & Distributions

**3.1 Allocation of Profits and Losses.** All items of Company income, gain, loss, deduction, and credit shall be allocated **fifty percent (50%) to Member Cummins and fifty percent (50%) to Member Karsor**.

**3.2 Distributions.** Distributions of available cash shall be made **quarterly**, in proportion to Percentage Interests, upon the unanimous consent of the Members, after establishing reasonable reserves for: (a) operating expenses for the following ninety (90) days; (b) the carrier quick-pay float; (c) estimated federal and state tax obligations of the Members attributable to Company income; and (d) the surety bond and insurance renewal obligations.

**3.3 Tax Distributions.** Notwithstanding §3.2, the Company shall use commercially reasonable efforts to distribute to each Member, no later than April 1 of each year, an amount sufficient to cover such Member's estimated tax liability on allocated Company income for the prior year.

**3.4 Partner Draws.** The Members may establish equal monthly draws by unanimous written consent (initially **$1,250.00 per Member per month**, commencing when Company monthly gross margin first exceeds $4,000). Draws are advances against distributions.

**3.5 Tax Classification.** The Company shall be taxed as a **partnership** (IRS Form 1065; Schedule K-1 to each Member) unless the Members unanimously elect S-Corporation status. The Members intend to evaluate an S-Corp election effective January 1, 2027.

## Article IV · Management

**4.1 Member-Managed.** The Company is **member-managed** under Minn. Stat. §322C.0407. Each Member has equal rights in the management and conduct of the Company's business.

**4.2 Division of Responsibilities.** Day-to-day responsibilities are divided as follows (each Member acting with authority within their domain, subject to §4.3):

| Domain | Lead |
| --- | --- |
| Shipper sales, pricing, load operations, carrier vetting, compliance calendar | Oliver Cummins |
| Technology (Command Deck development & maintenance), brand, media, marketing | Daniel W. Karsor |
| Finance, banking, capital decisions | Joint |

**4.3 Major Decisions — Unanimous Consent Required.** Notwithstanding anything to the contrary, the following actions require the **unanimous written consent of both Members**:
  (a) any expenditure, commitment, or series of related expenditures exceeding **$2,500**;
  (b) incurring any indebtedness, guaranty, or lien;
  (c) hiring or terminating any employee or engaging any contractor with aggregate annual compensation over $10,000;
  (d) admitting a new member or issuing any equity or profit interest;
  (e) selling, licensing, or encumbering the Command Deck or any material Company IP;
  (f) entering, amending, or terminating any factoring, lending, or banking facility;
  (g) any transaction between the Company and a Member or a Member's affiliate (including Member Karsor's barbershop or podcast studio businesses);
  (h) settling any claim or litigation over $5,000;
  (i) amending the Articles of Organization or this Agreement;
  (j) filing bankruptcy, dissolving, merging, or selling substantially all assets;
  (k) releasing or repurposing the Owner Launch Runway Reserve described in the Business Plan §10.2.

**4.4 Banking.** All Company funds shall be held in accounts in the Company's name. Any single disbursement over **$2,500** requires the signature or documented electronic approval of **both Members**.

**4.5 Devotion of Time.** Each Member shall devote such time as is reasonably necessary to perform their responsibilities. The Members acknowledge that (i) Member Cummins maintains W-2 employment at Tennant Companies until the Company reaches a sustained 60-load month, and (ii) Member Karsor continues to own and operate his barbershop and podcast studio businesses in Brooklyn Park, MN. Neither outside activity shall constitute a breach of this Agreement, subject to Article IX.

**4.6 No Compensation for Management.** Except for draws and distributions under Article III, no Member shall receive salary for services as a Member unless unanimously approved.

## Article V · Books, Records & Reporting

**5.1 Books.** The Company shall maintain complete books and records at its principal office and within the Orisei Brokerage Command Deck accounting module, on an accrual basis, consistent with GAAP.

**5.2 Fiscal Year.** The fiscal year shall be the calendar year.

**5.3 Reports.** The Company shall deliver to each Member: (a) monthly management accounts (P&L, cash position, capital-account status); (b) quarterly partner-review packages (KPIs vs. Business Plan §13); and (c) annual K-1s no later than March 15.

**5.4 Inspection.** Each Member has full access to all books, records, bank accounts, and Command Deck data at all times.

---

## Article VI · Deadlock Resolution

**6.1 Deadlock Defined.** A "Deadlock" exists when the Members are unable to reach unanimous consent on a Major Decision (§4.3) after good-faith discussion for **thirty (30) days**.

**6.2 Escalation Ladder.**
  (a) **Negotiation.** The Members shall meet in person at least twice within the 30-day period.
  (b) **Mediation.** If unresolved, the Members shall submit the dispute to a neutral mediator in **Hennepin County, Minnesota**, splitting costs equally, within thirty (30) days thereafter.
  (c) **Buy-Sell ("Shotgun") Procedure.** If mediation fails, either Member (the "Offeror") may deliver a written offer stating a single price for a 50% interest. The other Member (the "Offeree") must, within **forty-five (45) days**, elect either to (i) **sell** their entire interest to the Offeror at that price, or (ii) **buy** the Offeror's entire interest at that same price. Closing shall occur within sixty (60) days of election, payable up to 50% at closing and the balance by promissory note over no more than twenty-four (24) months at the applicable federal rate.

---

## Article VII · Transfer Restrictions

**7.1 General Prohibition.** No Member may sell, assign, pledge, or otherwise transfer any interest in the Company, in whole or in part, without the prior written consent of the other Member, except as provided in this Article.

**7.2 Right of First Refusal.** If a Member receives a bona fide third-party offer, the other Member shall have **sixty (60) days** to purchase the offered interest on the same terms. Any permitted transferee receives only economic rights unless admitted as a member by unanimous consent (Minn. Stat. §322C.0502).

**7.3 Involuntary Transfers.** Any interest transferred by operation of law (divorce, bankruptcy, creditor process) shall be subject to a Company/Member purchase option at fair value determined under §8.2.

---

## Article VIII · Death, Disability & Withdrawal

**8.1 Triggering Events.** Upon a Member's (a) death, (b) permanent disability (inability to perform duties for 120 consecutive days), or (c) voluntary withdrawal with ninety (90) days' written notice, the remaining Member shall have the option to purchase the affected interest.

**8.2 Valuation.** Purchase price shall be the **fair market value** of the interest determined by an independent business appraiser jointly selected (or, failing agreement, by the average of two appraisals, one selected by each side), taking into account the Company's book of shipper contracts, the Command Deck IP, and working capital, without minority or marketability discounts.

**8.3 Payment Terms.** Ten percent (10%) at closing; balance by promissory note in equal monthly installments over **twenty-four (24) months** at the applicable federal rate, secured by the purchased interest.

**8.4 Life Insurance.** The Members may, by unanimous consent, cause the Company to purchase cross-purchase life insurance to fund obligations under this Article.

---

## Article IX · Non-Competition, Non-Solicitation & Confidentiality

**9.1 Non-Compete.** During membership and for **eighteen (18) months** thereafter, no Member shall, within the United States, directly or indirectly own, operate, or work for a competing property freight brokerage, or broker freight outside the Company. Member Karsor's barbershop and podcast studio businesses, and Member Cummins' W-2 employment at Tennant Companies (subject to the conflict wall in Business Plan §12), are expressly excluded and permitted.

**9.2 Non-Solicitation.** For eighteen (18) months after ceasing to be a Member, a former Member shall not solicit the Company's shippers, carriers, or employees.

**9.3 Confidentiality.** Each Member shall hold in strict confidence all non-public Company information, including shipper lists, lane pricing, margin data, and Command Deck source code, during membership and for five (5) years thereafter.

**9.4 IP Ownership.** All software, content, media, brand assets, and work product created by either Member in connection with the Company's business (including Command Deck enhancements authored by Member Karsor and podcast/media content produced for the Company) is and shall remain the **sole property of the Company** as work made for hire, and each Member hereby assigns all such rights to the Company.

**9.5 Reasonableness / Blue Pencil.** The Members agree these covenants are reasonable; a court may reform any provision found overbroad to the maximum enforceable scope under Minnesota law.

---

## Article X · Indemnification & Insurance

**10.1 Indemnification.** The Company shall indemnify each Member to the fullest extent permitted by Minn. Stat. §322C.0408 for acts within the scope of Company duties, except for acts of gross negligence, willful misconduct, fraud, or knowing violation of law (including any violation of 49 CFR Part 371 broker regulations).

**10.2 Insurance.** The Company shall maintain at minimum: $1,000,000 general liability; $100,000 contingent cargo; $1,000,000 errors & omissions (broker's liability); and the FMCSA-mandated BMC-84 $75,000 surety bond.

---

## Article XI · Representations of the Members

Each Member represents and warrants that: (a) they have full capacity and authority to execute this Agreement; (b) their execution does not violate any other agreement binding on them; (c) they are acquiring their interest for their own account and not for resale; and (d) they have had the opportunity to consult independent legal and tax counsel regarding this Agreement.

---

## Article XII · Dissolution & Winding Up

**12.1 Events of Dissolution.** The Company shall dissolve only upon: (a) unanimous written consent of the Members; (b) entry of a decree of judicial dissolution under Minn. Stat. §322C.0701; or (c) the sale of all or substantially all Company assets.

**12.2 Winding Up.** Upon dissolution, assets shall be applied in the following order: (i) creditors, including Members as creditors; (ii) reserves the winding-up Members reasonably deem necessary; (iii) to the Members in accordance with positive capital-account balances; and (iv) any remainder 50/50.

**12.3 Command Deck on Dissolution.** Unless the Members unanimously agree otherwise, upon dissolution the Command Deck IP shall be offered first to Member Cummins at appraised value, with sale proceeds distributed per §12.2.

---

## Article XIII · General Provisions

**13.1 Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the **State of Minnesota**, without regard to conflicts-of-law principles.

**13.2 Dispute Forum.** Subject to Article VI, exclusive venue for any action lies in the state or federal courts sitting in **Hennepin County, Minnesota**, and each Member consents to personal jurisdiction there.

**13.3 Entire Agreement.** This Agreement, together with the Articles of Organization and the Business Plan (v2.0) referenced herein, constitutes the entire agreement of the Members and supersedes all prior understandings.

**13.4 Amendment.** This Agreement may be amended only by a writing signed by **both Members**.

**13.5 Severability.** If any provision is held invalid, the remainder shall continue in full force.

**13.6 Notices.** Notices shall be in writing and delivered by hand, certified mail, or email with confirmation, to the addresses on file with the Company.

**13.7 Counterparts & Electronic Signature.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original.

**13.8 Spousal Consent.** If applicable, each Member shall cause their spouse to execute a spousal consent acknowledging the transfer restrictions herein.
